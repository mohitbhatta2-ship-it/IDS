# Software Engineering (ENCT 352): Chapter-wise Past Questions

Source: TU IOE BCT III/I Software Engineering (CT 601) papers, **2068 Chaitra to 2083 Baishakh** (27 papers, regular and back), sorted into the chapters of the new **ENCT 352** syllabus.

**How to read this**
- Similar questions from different years are combined into one entry. The years it was asked are in `[ ]` at the end.
- ⭐ = asked 5 or more times. Study these first.
- Case-study DFD, use-case and FR/NFR questions are listed with their scenario so you can practise them.
- Old CT 601 topics that are **not** in ENCT 352 (real-time systems, software reuse/CBSE, COCOMO/FP estimation) are in the **Appendix** at the end. Check with your teacher whether they can still come.
